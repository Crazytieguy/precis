  13→beets
  14→=====
  15→
  16→Beets is the media library management system for obsessive music geeks.
  18→The purpose of beets is to get your music collection right once and for all. It
  19→catalogs your collection, automatically improving its metadata as it goes. It
  20→then provides a suite of tools for manipulating and accessing your music.
  21→
  22→Here's an example of beets' brainy tag corrector doing its thing:
  23→
  24→::
  25→
  26→    $ beet import ~/music/ladytron
  27→    Tagging:
  28→        Ladytron - Witching Hour
  29→    (Similarity: 98.4%)
  30→     * Last One Standing      -> The Last One Standing
  31→     * Beauty                 -> Beauty*2
  32→     * White Light Generation -> Whitelightgenerator
  33→     * All the Way            -> All the Way...

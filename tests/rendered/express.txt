  117→## Philosophy
  118→
  119→  The Express philosophy is to provide small, robust tooling for HTTP servers, making
  120→  it a great solution for single page applications, websites, hybrids, or public
  121→  HTTP APIs.
  122→
  123→  Express does not force you to use any specific ORM or template engine. With support for over
  124→  14 template engines via [@ladjs/consolidate](https://github.com/ladjs/consolidate),
  125→  you can quickly craft your perfect framework.
